# TIL 2026-11-04: NEC Box Fill Volume & Working Space Math

## 1. Box Fill Volume (NEC 314.16)
- Each conductor originating outside box: 1 volume allowance (based on Table 314.16(B)).
- Internal cable clamp: 1 volume allowance (based on largest conductor present).
- Device strap (switch/duplex receptacle): 2 volume allowances (based on largest conductor connected).
- Grounding conductors: 1 volume allowance for up to 4 grounds; 1/4 allowance for each ground conductor beyond 4.
- Conductor volume units: 14 AWG = $2.00\text{ in}^3$, 12 AWG = $2.25\text{ in}^3$, 10 AWG = $2.50\text{ in}^3$.

## 2. Working Space Clearances (NEC 110.26)
- Width: Minimum $30\text{ inches}$ or width of equipment (whichever is greater).
- Depth: 0–150V requires $3.0\text{ ft}$; 151–600V requires $3.0\text{ ft}$ (Condition 1), $3.5\text{ ft}$ (Condition 2 against grounded concrete/tile), or $4.0\text{ ft}$ (Condition 3 across live parts).
- Dedicated headspace: $6.5\text{ ft}$ minimum headroom.
